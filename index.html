About Karnataka Rajyotsava

Karnataka Rajyotsava is celebrated every year on 1 November. It marks the formation of Karnataka and celebrates Kannada language, culture and identity.

📅

A Day to Remember

On 1 November 1956, Kannada-speaking regions were brought together to form Mysore State. It was renamed Karnataka in 1973.

❤️

Our Language, Our Pride

Kannada has a rich literary history. Rajyotsava is a chance to celebrate our language and pass it on to future generations.

🤝

Unity in Diversity

People come together to celebrate Karnataka’s traditions, achievements, art, music and the many communities that make our state special.
